## Reconciliation note (impl vs PHASE12_PLAN_REFRESH §12.3)

- **I9.a / I9.d**: the shipped cascade masks privacy surface by replacing the
  whole JSONB payload with `{"redacted": true, "forget_event_id": <n>}` rather
  than setting a per-field `text`/`caption` value to the literal
  `[CONTENT_REDACTED: forget_event_id={n}]` quoted in the plan. Same privacy
  outcome (content removed, forget_event_id recorded); the binding tests assert
  the shipped contract.
- **I9.b**: the spec's distinct `rejection_reason='source_card_forgotten'` and
  auto `followup_correction` on already-executed actions are **not** implemented
  (cascade uses `source_forgotten` + payload redaction). Deferred to Phase 12.5.
